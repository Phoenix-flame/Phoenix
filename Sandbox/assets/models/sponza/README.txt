Crytek Sponza (glTF 2.0)
========================

Loaded by the Sponza showcase (File > Showcases > Sponza) from
assets/models/sponza/Sponza.gltf.

Source: Khronos glTF Sample Models, 2.0/Sponza/glTF
  https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Models/tree/main/2.0/Sponza

Files: Sponza.gltf (scene + materials), Sponza.bin (geometry: 262k triangles,
103 primitives) and 69 textures (1024x1024 base colour, normal and
metallic-roughness maps; foliage, chain and plant base colours carry an alpha
mask, alphaMode MASK). The engine uses the base colour and normal maps and
alpha-tests the masked materials; metallic-roughness maps are not read.
